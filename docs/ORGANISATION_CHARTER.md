# 24/7Nation Global Media — Organisation Charter

**Tagline:** Rising A Voice For The North  
**Movement:** One Vision • One Family • One Movement  
**Reach:** Arewa to the World

## 1. Purpose

24/7Nation Global Media is the parent organisation and shared operating identity for a connected media, entertainment, community and digital-platform ecosystem. It exists to discover talent, amplify responsible stories, create opportunities and connect Northern Nigerian creativity and culture with wider audiences.

This charter is the source of truth for the organisation's public structure and product boundaries. Website copy, dashboard labels, project plans and future apps must remain consistent with it.

## 2. The six official pillars

The organisation has exactly six official pillars:

1. **24/7 Nation Music Records** — artist development, music releases, talent management, promotion and music-rights administration.
2. **24/7 Nation Global Media** — the central media operation for news, journalism, entertainment coverage, interviews, podcasts, live media, partnerships and storytelling. **Maidammanation Productions** is the production and creative arm within this pillar.
3. **24/7 Nation Family** — the family and community movement for unity, youth empowerment, mutual support and community development.
4. **24/7 Nation Mobile App** — the planned mobile product for content discovery, artist updates, services and community connection.
5. **24/7 Nation Website** — the official public digital home for verified company information, news, music, videos, events and enquiries.
6. **Community Portal for Members** — the planned authenticated member space for profiles, announcements, programmes and member services.

These six names and their order must not be casually changed, split or expanded.

## 3. Important organisational boundaries

- **Maidammanation Productions** is a department/production arm inside pillar 2. It is not a seventh pillar and must not be presented as a separate parent company.
- **24/7 Nation Connect** is a communication service planned to work across the website, member portal and future mobile app. It is not a seventh pillar.
- The six pillars sit under 24/7Nation Global Media as one organisation and shared brand.
- Do not describe an unregistered division, service or project as a separate incorporated legal entity.
- Public copy must distinguish active services from planned services.

## 4. Operating principles

1. **Truth before promotion:** never publish fabricated facts, fake metrics, unverified claims or invented achievements.
2. **Editorial responsibility:** a named human editor approves public journalism and corrections.
3. **Respect and safeguarding:** protect minors, vulnerable people, private information and community members.
4. **Rights and consent:** obtain appropriate permission for music, images, video, interviews, releases and third-party material.
5. **Security by default:** least-privilege access, strong authentication, protected secrets and auditable privileged actions.
6. **Accountability:** important decisions, approvals, corrections and access changes should leave a reliable record.
7. **Accessibility and inclusion:** build mobile-first, readable experiences and plan for multilingual audiences.
8. **Sustainable growth:** launch dependable core services before adding complex features.

## 5. Governance model

The founder/CEO is the organisation's highest platform authority. The platform should support delegated operational roles without transferring ownership of the organisation.

Proposed platform roles (to be implemented and formally approved before staff access is enabled):

- **Owner/CEO:** full platform oversight, owner account and final authority.
- **Super Administrator:** platform administration under explicit owner-granted scope; cannot change or remove the owner.
- **Department Administrator:** manages assigned department only.
- **Editor/Journalist:** creates and edits assigned editorial content; publication requires the defined approval workflow.
- **Artist/Artist Manager:** manages permitted artist profile and release information.
- **Production Team:** manages assigned production projects and approved assets.
- **Community Manager:** manages member applications and community programmes within safeguarding rules.
- **Member:** accesses only their own profile and authorised member services.

No staff role should be granted broad database access by default. Roles must be enforced by trusted backend checks and Firestore security rules, not merely by hiding buttons in the browser.

## 6. Publishing and approval

- Draft, review, approved, scheduled, published and archived are distinct content states.
- Public publishing requires an authorised human approval.
- Corrections must preserve the original record and capture the reason, approver and time.
- AI output is a draft or assistant result, never automatic editorial authority.
- Rights, source notes, consent and attribution must be recorded where relevant.

## 7. Product status language

Use these terms consistently:

- **Live:** implemented, deployed and tested for the stated user journey.
- **Beta:** implemented and available to a limited, clearly identified test group.
- **In development:** implementation is underway but not ready for normal use.
- **Planned:** approved direction, not yet implemented.
- **Preview/demo:** illustrative interface or test data; not an operational service.

Never label a placeholder, mock metric or planned feature as live.

## 8. Change control

A change that affects the six pillars, role authority, data ownership, privacy, money, publishing rights or public brand promises requires explicit owner approval and a written decision record. Update this charter when an approved structural decision changes.
